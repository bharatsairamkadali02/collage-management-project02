Role Permissions Matrix

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) security boundaries configured across all tiers

Admin (Superuser)

Complete system and operational authority.

  • All academic entity CRUD
  • Faculty & Student account registration
  • Fee management & payments
  • Audit logs & system configuration
  • Publish official transcripts
Faculty / Professor

Broad academic authority scoped to assigned classes.

  • Mark & update classroom attendance
  • Create & schedule MCQ examinations
  • Enter internal & external marks
  • Upload assignments & study materials (S3)
  • Cannot create login accounts or edit fees
Student

Restricted personal view-only and exam participation access.

  • View personal attendance analytics
  • Attempt scheduled MCQ exams
  • View semester published grades
  • Submit assignments & download notes
  • No access to other students or answers
